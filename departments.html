Our Departments

Explore the academic departments that make up our university community.

Department of Biology

Study about life, its origin, ecology, botany and Zoology.

Programs: BS Botany, BS Zoology, and BS Biotechnology

Department of Chemistry

Study chemical reactions, organic compounds and do lab research.

Programs: BS Chemistry and BS Biochemistry

Department of Physics

Study the fundamental laws of energy, matter and spacetime.

Programs: BS Physics, BS Electronics and BS Astrophysics

Department of Mathematics

Study mathematical theory, analytical thinking, statistics, and quantitative methods.

Programs: BS Mathematics and BS Statistics

Department of Computer Science

Learn computing, software development, information technology, and emerging technologies.

Programs: BS Computer Science, BS Information Technology, BS Software Engineering and BS Artificial Intelligence

Department of Media & Mass Communication

Learn jouranlism, film, broadcasting and public relations.

Programs: BA Jouranlism and BS Mass Communication

Department of Business Administration

Provides education in management, marketing, finance, entrepreneurship, and business operations.

Programs: BBA and BS Business Management

Department of Commerce

Learn corporate finance, banking, and auditing.

Programs: B.Com and BS Accounting & finance

Department of Arts

Learn painting, designing visual arts and sculptures.

Programs: BA Fine Arts

Department of English

Develops skills in language, literature, communication, and critical thinking.

Programs: BA English Litrerature and BA English Linguistics
