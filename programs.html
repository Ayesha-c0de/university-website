Programs & Degrees

Explore the academic programs and degree opportunities offered by our university.

Undergraduate Programs

BS Botany

A program to study plant life, from microscopic algae to giant trees.

BS Zoology

A program focused on animal biology, their behavior, evolution and ecosystems.

BS Biotechnology

A program to learn using living organisms and biological systems to develop useful products and technologies.

BS Chemistry

A program to understand the matter, its properties, composition and chemical reactions.

BS Biochemistry

A program to explore the chemical processes occcuring within living organisms.

BS Physics

A program for studying matter, energy, space, time, and fundamental laws of nature.

BS Electronics

A program focused on electrical circuits, electronic devices, and hardware system design.

BS Astrophysics

A program teaching how to apply physics principles to understand stars, planets, galaxies, and the universe.

BS Mathematics

A program for the study of logic, numbers, structures, shapes, and data patterns.

BS Statistics

A program teaching the science of collecting, analyzing, interpreting, and presenting numerical data.

BS Computer Science

A program focused on the study of computation, algorithms, software theory, and architecture.

BS Information Technology

A program for managing, maintaining, and securing computer systems, networks, and databases

BS Software Engineering

A program for applying engineering principles to design, build, and test software applications.

BS Artificial Intelligence

A program focused on designing smart systems that mimic human intelligence, learning, and reasoning.

BS Mass Communication

A program to learn how media impacts society and strategies for public communication.

BA Journalism

A program teaching the practice of reporting, writing, and editing news for media outlets.

BBA

A program exploring broad overview of managing business operations, strategy, and leadership.

BS Business Management

A program focused specifically on supervising teams, organizing resources, and optimizing business efficiency.

B.Com

A program to focus on the principles of trade, business law, and commercial operations.

BS Accounting and Finance

A program for tracking financial transactions and managing investments, risk, and wealth.

BA Fine Arts

A program that develops practical and creative skills in visual arts like painting, sculpture, and design.

BA English Literature

A program analyzing and interpreting written works, poetry, and drama across history.

BA English Linguistics

A program exploring the scientific study of language structure, grammar, sounds, and evolution.
